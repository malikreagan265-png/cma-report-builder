Pricing Strategy

Three options.One choice.

Instead of "here's the price" followed by "but I want more" — three genuine strategies with clear trade-offs, so the seller makes a decision rather than an objection.

Aggressive
Test the ceiling
The price that maximises upside. For sellers with no timeline pressure and a property that genuinely outperforms the comps. Comes with the risk of extended DOM and price reduction stigma.
Market
What the data supports
The price comparable properties have achieved. Generates strong buyer interest without leaving money on the table. The right strategy for most sellers in most markets.
Conservative
Speed and certainty
The price that prioritises a fast, clean close. Often generates multiple offers above asking. For sellers with a hard deadline or who need certainty over maximum return.
